Five-figure business before turning 18

I started in the business world at 16, and after trying several models, I joined forces with a friend and we founded an agency where we built digital funnels to help companies in the interior of Portugal grow online. At the time, I gave up on going to university, and even before I was 18, we had already made five figures in revenue with our own business. However, despite being a co-founder, I decided to leave to take advantage of a better opportunity, but I love continuing to see the agency I founded grow even though I'm no longer involved in operations.
